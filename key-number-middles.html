Middles Around Key Numbers

NFL + college football spread middles, built to Broham's exact liked windows. A middle counts when its both-win margin set — every final margin where BOTH legs cash — sits inside one cluster below, OR when either leg sits exactly on a key number (favorite −3/−7/−10/−14/−21/−24, dog +3/+7/+10/+14/+21/+24). Flat legs can push: a push on one leg plus a win on the other counts as covered. Both legs always shown with book + price: the favorite leg and the dog leg.
