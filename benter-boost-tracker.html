How it works (Mack, Vol. 1 — model 1.5, Benter boost). Every live model's flag is re-priced against the market: the model's probability is blended with the market's vig-free implied probability in Benter's logit style, p_blend = sigmoid(w·logit(p_model) + (1−w)·logit(p_market)), and the blended edge decides the flag (paper, $100 flat, ≥ 5%). w = 0.5 is a documented prior, not a fit — no out-of-sample sample of (model prob, market prob, outcome) exists to fit it (graded flag histories are selection-biased and tiny; the Elo backtest carries no market probabilities). Per-model settled counts accumulate below for a future fit. Market probabilities are two-sided devigs (game lines or prop Over/Under re-priced from HaterPicks; DarkHorse snapshot when live); where a two-sided re-price fails, the single-side price is adjusted by the mean hold measured today and labelled. The per-model table shows raw edge vs blended edge and raw-rule vs blended-rule flag counts — which models the blend helps, and which it hurts. v1 limits: derivatives / stale props / synthetic hold have no priced flags to blend yet; props grade from the ESPN summary API, not Basketball-Reference.
