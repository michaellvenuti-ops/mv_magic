NFL Key Numbers — What Games Actually Land On

My own scan of the nflverse game log: every completed NFL game since 2010 — 4,499 games (regular season + playoffs), final scores through the 2025 season plus 2026 games already played. Cross-checked against published research and Spanky's Be Better Bettors catalog. Built Nov 10, 2026.
The headline: margins have a tower, totals have a plateau. 14.6% of all games are decided by exactly 3 points — nearly 1 in 7 — and 8.7% by exactly 7. No final total comes close to that kind of spike: the single most common total, 51, lands just 3.9% of the time. That's why spread middles live and die on single numbers, while totals middles need width.

Your seven strict key margins cover 40.8% of every game played since 2010. Two honest wrinkles: a margin of 6 lands 6.7% and 4 lands 5.1% — both more often than 10 (5.0%), 14 (5.0%), 17 (3.4%), 21 (2.5%) or 28 (1.5%). The set is a betting convention (3s and 7s are where the half-point is priced richest), not a pure frequency ranking.

Final totals — where games end (over/unders)

Top 10 totals combined cover only 33.5% of games spread across a wide range — totals land everywhere. And look at 42: just 1.69%, a valley between 41 (3.42%) and 43 (3.76%). Spanky called 42 a "dead number" where a half-point is worth little — the data says he's exactly right.

Team totals — one team's score

20 — the most common team score in footballThe next cluster: 17, 24, 27, 23
A single team scores exactly 20 points in 7.0% of team-games — the #1 team score, ahead of 17 (6.5%), 24 (6.5%), 27 (5.9%) and 23 (4.9%). Scoring combos (two TDs + two FGs, etc.) funnel teams onto 20, which is why the team-total middle Over 19.5 / Under 20.5 exists.

What Spanky says (Be Better Bettors catalog)

"Middle around key numbers — focus on 3s and 7s (and 10s); NFL totals 37 and 41 are the totals worth buying on/off; first-half 22/23 are big numbers."
— Spanky, Ep 2 "Half Point Importance" (Dec 4, 2019). Verdict from the data: 37 and 41 both sit in the top 5 totals since 2010. ✓
"Books charging only 10 cents to buy on/off the NFL 3 — when the half point is worth 20+ cents — create middling opportunities."
— Spanky, Ep 2. Fats (Ep 46, Mar 2021) adds: buy on/off the 3 at ~20¢ and split the juice evenly on both sides of a 2.5/3.5 middle.
"Know exactly what a half-point is worth around NFL key numbers 3 and 7… and on totals, know the dead numbers — e.g., 42 — where a half-point is worth little."
— Spanky, Ep 71, Twitter Q+A (Nov 2022). The scan confirms 42 is the deadest number in the 40s: 1.69%.
"NFL team totals land on 20 unusually often — bet Over 19.5 and Under 20.5 to middle the key number."
— Fats, Ep 46. Confirmed: 20 is the most common team score in the data (7.0%). ✓
"Key numbers are weakening — the 3 and 7 are less strong than a decade ago: the extra point moved back, teams go for two more often, overtime rules changed."
— Ray Marino, Ep 26 (Jun 2020). The landing rate for exactly-3 hasn't actually faded — 15.64% (2002–09), 14.23% (2010–19), 15.18% (2020–25) — but Marino's pricing point stands: books now charge full freight around 3, so the value moved even if the frequency didn't. Spanky's own Ep 2 rule covers it: recalculate half-point values as rules change instead of relying on old data.

Cross-check against published research

• OddsShopper (26 years, 6,967 regular-season games, 1999–2025): 41 and 44 are the most-hit totals (264 games each), and 51 has the strongest "lift" of any total — 1.81× its neighbors — matching this scan, where 51 is the #1 total since 2010.
• SportsBettingDime: the most common final totals are 41, 44, 43 and 47 — the same cluster this scan finds (51, 44, 43, 41, 37, 40, 47).
• 99bookmakers / Pro Football Reference long-run data: ~15% of games decided by exactly 3, ~9% by exactly 7, then 10, 6, 4, 14 — the same margin order as this scan.

So what — for middles

• Spread middles: one number can be worth a fortune — a window covering 3 alone catches ~14.6% of games; covering 3 and 7 catches ~23.4%.
• Totals middles: no single total is worth more than ~3.9%, so a totals middle needs a multi-number window (like the wide builds) to have real both-win equity — a one-number totals window is a lottery ticket.
• Team-total middles: 20 is the one genuine key number — a window on 20 catches 7.0% of team scores.
• Don't pay up for a half-point through 42 on a total, or through 15/18/22/25/29-type dead spots on team scores — the games skip those numbers.
