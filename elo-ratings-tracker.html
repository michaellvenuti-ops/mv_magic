Mack Elo team ratings — NBA, NFL, NHL, NCAAB, NCAAF. Paper only, $100 flat. Updated 2026-11-26 03:22 PM ET.
How it works (Mack, Vol. 2 — Elo team ratings, in Python). Every team starts at 1500. After each game: rating += K x (actual − expected), expected = 1 / (1 + 10−(gap + home edge)/400). K is sport-specific and tuned by backtest on the season so far (lowest Brier score over a K grid), capped at 32 — early-season Brier keeps falling to the grid edge because every team starts flat at 1500, so K above 32 is not a stable season-long fit; re-tune mid-season once ratings converge. Home edge in Elo points: NBA/NCAAB 100, NFL/NCAAF 65, NHL 55 (none on neutral courts). Win probability comes straight from the rating gap + home edge. Spread: expected margin = (gap + home edge) / divisor (NBA/NCAAB 30, NFL/NCAAF 28 — approximate linear maps); P(cover) at the book spread is Normal with sport sigma (NBA 12, NCAAB 11, NFL 13.5, NCAAF 15). A side with line L (its own perspective, favourite negative) covers when its margin exceeds −L, so P(cover) = Φ((margin + L) / sigma). NHL is moneyline only — Elo does not predict totals, and the puck line is a fixed ±1.5. No model here predicts totals (Total Projectors covers totals). A game flags when the model's edge over the book's implied probability is ≥ 5%. Paper only — $100 flat per flag at the posted price, graded the next morning. Lines: DarkHorse snapshot when live; until then HaterPicks game pages for NBA only (best price per side at placeable books, labelled on every flag) — the other four sports print ratings only and flag nothing. Not modelled in v1: margin-of-victory scaling of the Elo update (win/loss-only Elo); a MOV multiplier is a v2 refinement once the backtest shows it helps. The first run shipped the spread cover sign backwards (Φ((margin − L)/sigma), inflating favourites) and it was fixed 2026-11-23 before any board publish.
