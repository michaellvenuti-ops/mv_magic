Push-Rate Charts

Updated November 23, 2026 by Mac

Logic L5 — how often games land on each margin, by league. Reference only, no bets. Updated 2026-11-23 06:30 PM ET.
How it works (Logic L5 — push-rate charts). Miller & Davidow price related markets from a strong market using a chart of how often games land on each margin. This page is that chart, built from ~10 calendar years of ESPN final scores (2016–2025), one scoreboard call per league per year. Margin is home minus away (a push lands when the final margin equals the spread). Segment shares are the average share of total scoring in each period (from ESPN linescores) — the derivative model scales a full-game total/spread by these shares to price 1Q/1H/1P lines, and the synthetic-hold scanner and xG model use the margin chart to price alt lines without pretending a push is impossible. This is a reference build, not a betting model: no flags, no record, no grading. Refresh weekly (Sundays); the data file push_rate_data.json is what the other models import (push_rates.py: p_cover, fair_alt_total, segment_shares). v1 caveat, stated plainly: ESPN caps a dates=YYYY call at 1,000 events and ignores page, so each capped year contributes its first 1,000 events only (NBA/NHL years run roughly Jan–Nov; NCAAB years are January-heavy; NCAAF 2017–18 failed with 504s and are absent). Year-month slicing (dates=YYYYMM) is proven to work and is the v2 fix for full-season coverage — the charts below are usable at n≈38k games but are not a complete 10-season census.

Source: ESPN public scoreboard API, calendar years 2016–2025. Built 2026-11-23.
