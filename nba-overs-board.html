NBA Overs & Unders Board

Daily NBA Over leans from @linemakersports' three-filter system, plus the Under mirror. Updated every morning; graded the next morning.

How it works

A game is an Over lean ONLY when both teams meet all three criteria on season-to-date stats: (1) Top 10 in offensive efficiency, (2) BOTTOM 10 in defensive efficiency (ranks 21-30 of 30 — bad defenses), (3) Top 10 in possessions per game (pace). The reel states no threshold beyond these ranks and no win-rate claim — none is claimed here either; grading below is the honest record.

A game is an Under lean ONLY when both teams meet all three mirrored criteria: (1) BOTTOM 10 in offensive efficiency (ranks 21-30), (2) TOP 10 in defensive efficiency (ranks 1-10), (3) BOTTOM 10 in possessions per game (ranks 21-30).

The Under side is Chet's mirror of Frank's system — Frank only published the Over version. The Under side starts with no track record and is graded separately from the Over side, never mixed.

Early-season caveat: season-to-date ranks are noisy in the first weeks of the season, when teams have played only a handful of games. A rank earned over 2-4 games can swing wildly after one night.
