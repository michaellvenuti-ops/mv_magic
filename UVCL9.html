Lab Manual

  • Layout: Cards are displayed from Pos 52 (Top Left) to Pos 1 (Bottom Right).
  • Faro OUT: Anchors Pos 52 and Pos 1. Ideal for preserving stacks.
  • Faro IN: Interleaves the top card into the 2nd position.
  • Stack Coach: Click a card to see its Faro destination and 8-shuffle cycle.
  • Poker Test: Simulates a 6-player deal. Toggle Bottom Deal to force the 5th card from the bottom of the deck.
