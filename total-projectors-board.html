Total Projectors Board — NCAAB + NCAAF

Frank Civittolo's (@linemakersports) two formula total projectors: build your own number for each team, add them, compare to the book's total. Updated every morning; graded the next morning, per sport.

How it works — NCAAB Totals Projector

From Frank's Nov 2, 2026 reel "Easiest Way to Predict an NCAAB Total!": for each team, compute an offense-based estimate (the team's offensive efficiency x possessions) and a defense-based estimate (the opponent's defensive efficiency — points it allows per possession — x possessions), average the two, and add both teams' numbers for the projected game total. His whiteboard example: Illinois ~77.5 and Purdue ~74.4, a ~151.9 total. In formula form, per team: own possessions/game x (own offensive efficiency + opponent defensive efficiency) / 2.

Input adaptation: Frank names the inputs (offensive efficiency, defensive efficiency, possessions per game) but no stats site. This board uses BartTorvik's adjusted efficiency (AdjOE / AdjDE, points per 100 possessions) and adjusted tempo (AdjT, possessions per 40 minutes) — the closest public equivalents to Frank's inputs. Early-season caveat: Torvik's ratings blend his preseason priors with results — in the season's first days, when most teams have played 0-1 games, they lean on the priors and become results-driven as games accumulate.

How it works — NCAAF Totals Projector

From Frank's Oct 21, 2026 reel "Easiest way to predict NCAAF total!": the same shape with per-play rates — per team, own plays/game x (own points per play + points per play the opponent allows) / 2. His whiteboard example (Tennessee @ Alabama): Alabama (0.656 scored + 0.251 Tennessee allows) / 2 x 67.3 plays = 30.5; Tennessee (0.427 + 0.318 allowed by Alabama) / 2 x 71.2 plays = 26.5; projected total ~57. Inputs are TeamRankings' public season-to-date points per play, opponent points per play, and plays per game — the named public equivalents of Frank's inputs.

Flag thresholds are CHET'S DISPLAY CHOICES, NOT Frank's claims — neither reel states a threshold or a win-rate claim, and none is claimed here; the grading below is the honest record. NCAAB: flag OVER when the projected total sits at least 5.0 points ABOVE the book total, UNDER when at least 5.0 BELOW. NCAAF: the same at a 3.0-point gap. Games inside the band are shown but never flagged.

Not the Football Totals board: this page is a pure formula-projection system. The Football Totals board (totals-board.html) is a separate signal system — elite defenses by points allowed per play, line movement from the open, and splits divergence — with its own scorecard. The two systems are deliberately kept separate and are never merged.
