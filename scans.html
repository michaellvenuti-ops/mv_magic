Hung Number Board

A NJ book 1.0+ points off the median spread, 1.5+ off the median total, or 5+ implied-probability points off on a moneyline. A lone hung number is the play; a book off everywhere is opinionated pricing — leave it.

Reverse Line Movement Board

70%+ of tickets on one side AND the median line moving 1.0+ (spread) / 1.5+ (total) toward the minority side. RLM is a signal, not proof of sharp money.

Leader-Follower Board

Logging phase: a book earns a LEAD when it moves 1.0+ / 1.5+ while 3+ books still show the old number and 3+ follow by the next check. Alerts only after a book reaches 3 leads in 14 days. Board display timing is not proof of who truly moved first.
