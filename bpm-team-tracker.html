Mack NBA BPM bottom-up team model — every team, from player ratings. Paper only, $100 flat. Updated 2026-11-24 09:44 AM ET.
How it works (Mack, Vol. 2 — NBA BPM bottom-up team model, in Python). For each team, the top-8 rotation by minutes per game (Basketball-Reference per-game stats) and each man's Box Plus/Minus (advanced stats). Team strength = minutes-weighted average BPM of the rotation. Expected margin = (home BPM − away BPM) x 1.0 + 2.7 home edge (0 on neutral courts). The 1.0 slope is a v1 calibration: BPM is points above average per 100 possessions and NBA pace is ~100, so a minutes-weighted BPM gap reads directly as a points-per-game gap; the state file records model margin vs actual margin every day so the slope can be calibrated from results later. Win probability = Normal CDF of the model margin (sigma 12); spread probability = Normal CDF at the book spread (same sigma). Total comes from the NBA Overs scan's daily team-stats snapshot (ORtg / DRtg / pace), Normal sigma 12. Starters are VERIFIED from each team's last 2 box scores — a recent starter outside the top-8 by minutes joins the rotation. Injuries from ESPN: an OUT rotation player is REMOVED and the next man by minutes takes his slot; a QUESTIONABLE top-3 player is flagged WITH a tag. Guardrail: if a top-3 rotation player is OUT and his replacement's BPM is 3+ points worse (a depleted rotation), the edge bar rises from 5% to 7% for that team. A game flags when the model's edge over the book's implied probability is ≥ 5% (7% depleted). Paper only — $100 flat per flag at the posted price, graded the next morning. Lines: DarkHorse snapshot when live; until then HaterPicks game pages (best price per side at placeable books, labelled on every flag). Rejected fit, documented: a per-player OLS regression of BPM on counting stats was not used — BPM is already the fitted rating; regressing it again adds noise, not signal.
