Spread Cover Board — NBA + NCAAB

Frank Civittolo's (@linemakersports) spread-cover system for NBA and college basketball: follow the line movement and the sharp-money divergence. Updated every morning; graded the next morning, per sport.

How it works

Frank's factors: (1) line movement toward a team (his NBA example: the 76ers open -4.5 and move to -7.5; his NCAAB example: Duke opens -27 and moves to -31), and (2) money% vs bet% divergence — when the money is concentrated on the opposite side from the bet count, the money side is the sharp side (his examples: 76% of bets on the underdog but 75% of the money on the favorite; 65% of bets on Georgia Tech +31 but 70% of the money on Duke). The NBA version adds (3) a last-10 ATS trend (his example: 8-2 ATS); the NCAAB version has no ATS factor.

Frank states NO thresholds in either reel and makes NO win-rate claim for these systems — none is claimed here either; the grading below is the honest record. The flag rule is CHET'S DISPLAY CHOICE: a side is flagged when BOTH hold vs the game's stored opener — the spread has moved 1.0+ points toward that side, AND money% on that side exceeds bet% on that side by 10+ percentage points. NBA flags show the team's last-10 ATS record as context and are tagged '3-signal' at 7-3 ATS or better (also Chet's choice).

Splits source: ScoresAndOdds public consensus pages (% of Bets and % of Money), the same source the football public-fade scan uses — verified for basketball on 2026-11-03 (NCAAB live that day; the NBA page is the same consensus product and had no games that day, Election Day). Openers are stored at first sighting — the ScoresAndOdds published Open when shown, otherwise the first stored line.
