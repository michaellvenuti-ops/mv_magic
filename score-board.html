Score Board

Weekly NFL score predictions from the @linemakersports points-per-play method, next to the book's spread and total. Updated every Sunday morning; prior weeks stay below as history, with grading.

How it works — and what it does not prove

Per team, predicted score = (own offensive points per play x own plays per game + opponent's defensive points allowed per play x own plays per game) / 2, using 2026 season-to-date team stats. In plain terms: average what the team's own offense projects with what the opponent's defense projects for it.

Crude estimator, stated plainly: no opponent adjustments, no injuries, weather, or home field. Gaps of 3+ points are highlighted as a display choice — the reel states no threshold. A prediction is a lean to investigate, not a pick. Grading below (predicted vs actual error, and whether the prediction's lean beat the book's number) is how the method gets tested.
