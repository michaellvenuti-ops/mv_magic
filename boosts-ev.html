Odds Boosts EV Check (Chet)

Updated November 25, 2026 by Chet

Most odds boosts are fake value — the "boosted" price is often still worse than another book's unboosted price. Each boost below is devigged against the no-vig fair price ("perfect line"). Only EV% > 0 flags, and only when the fair price comes from real devigged prices (other books on the same market, or leg-by-leg for parlays). A boost whose fair price can only be estimated from the book's own regular price with an assumed hold is WATCH — unverified, never a flag. Most boosts fail. That is the point of the check.

Method

American → decimal: +a → 1 + a/100; −a → 1 + 100/|a|. Implied prob = 1 ÷ decimal. Fair prob (two-way) = (1/oA) ÷ (1/oA + 1/oB); multi-way generalises the same way. ML parlay fair prob = product of devigged leg fair probs. EV% = (boosted decimal × fair prob − 1) × 100. Flag bar: EV% > 0 with a fair price from devigged real prices. Profit-boost hedge: boosted profit = stake × (decimal − 1) × (1 + boost%); hedge stake = boosted payout ÷ hedge decimal. Daily scan 10:00 AM ET.
