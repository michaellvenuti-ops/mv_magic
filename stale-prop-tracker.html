How it works (Logic L7 — stale props, 5-step process). Props are priced off the main line at posting and often never updated after news moves the main line. v1 is day-over-day only: today's NBA PTS/REB/AST prop lines (HaterPicks, best price per side) are compared with yesterday's stored baseline. A prop flags when the game total moved ≥ 2.0 points (or the spread ≥ 1.5) since yesterday while the prop line is UNCHANGED, and the stale side's edge clears 5% — edge from a Normal model whose mean is shifted by the expected prop move (main-line move × the player's share of his team's scoring), sigma PTS 6 / REB 3.5 / AST 3 (documented assumptions). Paper only — $100 flat, graded the next morning from the ESPN boxscore. Intraday staleness is invisible to v1 (one snapshot/day); phase-2 30-min pulls would be needed to catch a prop going stale mid-day. The first run of the watch seeds the baseline and flags nothing.
