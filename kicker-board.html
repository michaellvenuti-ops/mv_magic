How the system works — and what it does not prove

A kicker qualifies for Over 3.5 kicker points (4+ points: a field goal plus an extra point, two field goals, or four extra points) when all three 2026 season-to-date filters hold: (1) the opposing defense allows 7+ kicker points per game; (2) the kicker's own offense averages 3+ red-zone attempts per game; (3) the kicker's field-goal accuracy is 90%+ on at least 10 FG attempts. The 10-attempt minimum is a stated guardrail against small-sample marks — the reel states no minimum.

The reel's “wins 90% of the time” is the creator's claim, unverified. Every qualifier below is graded after the games (record + $100 flat at the recorded price, record-only when no price was recorded), so the real record tests the claim. A qualifier is a lean to investigate, not a pick.
