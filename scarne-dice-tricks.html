The Three Dice Miracle

You name all three dice, in order, from one number the spectator gives you.

Performance: Hand over three dice. While your back is turned, the spectator rolls them (or picks three numbers), lines them up in order, and does this arithmetic on a phone calculator or paper, keeping the dice hidden:

  1. Take the first die and double it.
  2. Add 5.
  3. Multiply by 5.
  4. Add the second die.
  5. Multiply by 10.
  6. Add the third die.
  7. Announce the final total to you.
THE REVEAL: Silently subtract 250 from their total. The three digits of what's left are the three dice, in order. Name them one at a time.
Worked example — the dice are 3, 5, 2:
Double the first: 3 × 2 = 6 → add 5 = 11 → × 5 = 55 → add the second die: 55 + 5 = 60 → × 10 = 600 → add the third die: 600 + 2 = 602.
They tell you 602. You subtract 250 → 352. The dice were 3, 5, 2. ✔

Memory hook: the recipe is 2, +5, ×5, +die, ×10, +die — and your only job is minus 250. The arithmetic rebuilds the three numbers as the hundreds, tens, and ones digits, and the 250 is exactly the padding the recipe added along the way.

Dice Will Tell

Scarne on Card Tricks, No. 109 — credited to Thurston and Weigle. The dice themselves count down to the chosen card.

Performance and steps, in order:

  1. Have a card chosen, remembered, and returned — and get it to the top of the deck (your usual top control; the spectator suspects nothing).
  2. Deal 14 cards, one at a time, onto the top of the deck, burying the chosen card exactly 14 deep.
  3. Hand the spectator two dice and have them rolled, out in the open.
  4. Count down through the deck by the number showing on the tops of the two dice — deal that many cards, one by one.
  5. Turn the dice over in place and continue the count by the number on the bottoms, dealing on from where you stopped.
  6. The card you land on at the end of the count is the chosen card. Turn it over and take the bow.
WHY IT LANDS: opposite faces of a die always total 7, so the tops of two dice plus their bottoms always total 14 — tops + bottoms = 14, every roll, no exceptions. The count always travels exactly 14 cards, straight to the card you buried 14 deep.

Memory hook: 14 buries it, 14 finds it. Whatever the dice show, tops-then-bottoms adds to 14 — the dice do the counting and they can't miss.
