Virtual Card Lab — User Guide

PEEK MODE
🃏 Peek ModeFlips all cards face down. Click any card to reveal it for 1 second then it hides again. Use this to drill stack memorization — test yourself by guessing each card before you click to reveal it. Toggle off to return to normal view.
MECHANICS & SHUFFLES
Faro OUTPerfect weave — top card stays on top (position 1). 8 consecutive Out-Faros returns any deck to its original order.
Faro INPerfect weave — top card moves to position 2. Used for specific stack entry procedures.
Reverse ShuffleReverses the entire 52-card deck end to end.
Gilbreth ShuffleReverses the top 26 cards then randomly interleaves them with the bottom 26.
Cut Deck (26)Moves the top 26 cards to the bottom.
RandomizeFisher-Yates full randomization.
Shift LeftMoves the top card to the bottom.
Shift RightMoves the bottom card to the top.
Reverse Top 26Reverses only the top 26 cards. Bottom 26 untouched. Used in Spanish NDO ➔ Mnemonica procedure (Step 2).
Reverse Bottom 26Reverses only the bottom 26 cards. Top 26 untouched.
Unequal Faro Top 18Cuts top 18 and out-faros them into the top of the remaining 34. Used in Spanish NDO ➔ Mnemonica procedure (Step 3).
Cut at N 🧪Enter any number 1–51 and click 🧪 CUT to move exactly that many cards from top to bottom.
FALSE WORK (STACK PROTECTION)
Zarrow ShuffleSwaps top 2 with bottom 2. Cards 3–50 remain undisturbed.
Blind FaroMoves top 2 cards to positions 25–26 (center).
