Multi-Venue Price Dashboard (Chet)

Updated November 27, 2026 by Chet

Every game, every venue side by side — so the best price is visible when it is time to play. Kalshi and Polymarket are prediction exchanges (prices in cents per $1 payout); DraftKings is the book. Best price per outcome is highlighted. Prices move — snapshot only, verify in the app before placing. No picks, no orders.

Nothing is invented: every number below traces to the Kalshi scan, a live Polymarket market, Novig's public catalog, or DraftKings via ESPN.
How it works. Kalshi prices come from the Mac scanner (public Kalshi API, every 5 min) — Kalshi blocks browser requests, so its prices arrive as a data file in this repo. Novig prices come from Novig's public catalog, refreshed with the dashboard data (every 5 min). Polymarket prices are fetched LIVE in your browser straight from Polymarket's Gamma API (one batched call) — click any Polymarket price to see its live CLOB order book. Book prices are DraftKings via ESPN, as captured by the Kalshi scan. The "Best book" column shows the best price across the books in the dashboard data file. "Best" = lowest cost to buy $1 of payout on that exact outcome at that exact line; when Polymarket doesn't offer the book's exact line, its nearest lines are shown separately and never compared as if they were the same bet. Polymarket prices exclude its trading fee; Kalshi's 7% fee on winnings is not reflected in the ask. The Bet button copies a ticket ("BET — venue: game — market @ price — via Chet") to paste to Chet in chat — it places nothing. NBA / NHL games appear once the Kalshi scan covers those leagues.
