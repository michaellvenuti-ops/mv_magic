01: Naming the Card Drawn

Simplified: If someone picks a card, just look at the card on the very bottom of the deck. Add 3 to its number and pick the next suit in order (C-H-S-D). That's their card!

Example: Bottom is 9 of Diamonds. 9 + 3 = 12 (Queen). Next suit after Diamonds is Clubs. It's the Queen of Clubs.

By glancing at the bottom card, adding three to it, and calling the suit that follows the bottom card, you will name the card drawn. Example—A person draws a card and you find that the bottom card is the nine of Diamonds. You then add three to nine, which is twelve or the Queen, and call it the Queen of Clubs, as Clubs follow Diamonds.

02: Counting a Handful

Simplified: If someone grabs a chunk of cards, look at the bottom card and the same suit card near the top. Subtract the top number from the bottom number, multiply by 4, and subtract how many cards were above that top one. That's the total they held!
Subtract the number of the suit card on or nearest the top from that of the same suit as the bottom card, multiply the result by four and subtract the number of cards, including the suit card on top, and the result will be the number of cards drawn.

07: GPS Position

Simplified: To find where any card is, find the same suit card at the bottom. Subtract the card they want from your bottom card. Multiply by 4. Subtract how many cards are below that bottom card.
Subtract the number of the card called for from the number of the card of the same suit nearest the bottom, multiply the result by four, then subtract the number of cards (if any) below the bottom suit card.
