System Foundation

Trick Summary

Setup the deck in the following suit order: Clubs → Hearts → Spades → Diamonds. Every card must be exactly 3 values higher than the one before it (A=1, J=11, Q=12, K=13).

01: Naming the Card Drawn

Trick Summary

Peek the bottom card. Add 3 to the value and name the next suit in order.

02: Counting Handful

Trick Summary

Subtract top card from bottom, multiply by 4, minus cards above.

03: Pocket Handful Call

Trick Summary

Call via bottom peek (+3) or top card (-3) depending on card orientation.

04: Pocket Card Deduction

Trick Summary

Use elimination based on the suit/value you already know.

05: Thinking of a Card

Trick Summary

Hesitate on a card to force it, then move it to 4th from top.

06: Stop Anytime

Trick Summary

Name cards silently as you run them behind your back.

07: GPS Position

Trick Summary

(Bottom Match - Called Value) x 4, minus cards below match.

08: Dealing Trumps

Trick Summary

Cut to the trump suit and deal normally.

09: Dealing Every Heart

Trick Summary

Get a Heart on the bottom and deal.

10: Pat Flush

Trick Summary

In a 4-man game, cut the prior suit to bottom.

11: Bonus Mind Reading

Trick Summary

Secretly reverse 16 cards to locate a thought-of number.
