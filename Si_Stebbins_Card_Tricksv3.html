System Foundation

Setup the deck in the following suit order: Clubs (♣) → Hearts (♥) → Spades (♠) → Diamonds (♦).

Rule: Every card must be exactly 3 values higher than the one before it. (A=1, J=11, Q=12, K=13).

01: Naming the Card Drawn

Peek the bottom card. Add 3 to the value and name the next suit in order.

"By glancing at the bottom card, adding three to it, and calling the suit that follows the bottom card, you will name the card drawn."
