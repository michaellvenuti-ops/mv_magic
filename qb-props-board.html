QB Props Board

Weekly NFL tracking of two methods from @linemakersports (Frank Civittolo): high-hit QB same-game parlays and a completions-times-yards-per-completion yardage projection. Updated every Thursday morning; prior weeks stay below as history.

How the two methods work — and their limits

A. High-hit SGP. For each starting QB, over the trailing 20 regular-season games (minimum 12 to qualify), the JOINT hit rate — games where BOTH legs hit — for 1+ passing TD plus an Over alternate passing-yards line. Only combos at 90%+ joint are kept, and a combo is flagged only when the book's SGP price implies a probability at least 3 points below the joint rate. A past hit rate is not a future probability: a 100% rate on 12 games is weak evidence, and two −1200 legs parlay to about −576, so $50 wins only about $8.68 and one loss wipes roughly 5.8 wins.

B. Yardage projection. Projected yards = the QB's average completions per game (season to date) × the opposing defense's allowed yards per completion (season: passing yards allowed ÷ completions allowed). Flagged when the gap vs the book's main passing-yards line is 15+ yards. This is a crude estimator — game script, weather, injuries, and offensive-line play are not in it. A flag is a lean to investigate, not a pick.

Tread lightly: heavy-juice SGP winners get limited fast. You place every bet yourself; nothing here places a bet.
