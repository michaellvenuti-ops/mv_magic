How it works (Mack, Bayesian Sports Models in R — models 3.1/3.2). This model attacks the shape of the distribution, not the median: it compares the model's posterior SD against the SD the market's prices imply. Normal approximation, not full MCMC — R and Stan are not installed on the Mac, so the "posterior" is a Normal (NBA/NFL margin, from Elo ratings + the Elo spread sigma) or Poisson (NHL goals, from MoneyPuck xG lambdas) approximation built from the Batch 1/2 model outputs. A full R/Stan MCMC fit is the v2 upgrade if R+Stan ever lands on the Mac. Market-implied SD comes from the devigged moneyline + spread prices jointly (NBA/NFL: two equations solve the market's Normal margin) or from a Poisson-lambda grid fit on the devigged ML + total + puck-line prices (NHL). WIDE (our SD ≥ 1.15× market SD): tails are fatter than the market prices — the main-line side our posterior prices at ≥ 5% edge flags (paper, $100 flat), and the alt-line sell-points target is listed as a watchlist item with its break-even price. NARROW (our SD ≤ 0.85× market SD): a middle is the play — listed as a watchlist middle with its both-win range, probability under our posterior, and the maximum combined implied probability the legs can carry. Watchlist items are not priced flags: no alt-line price exists in any covered source until the DarkHorse snapshot carries alt-line depth. v1 limits: NBA/NFL totals are a gap (no total posterior without BPM, which needs Basketball-Reference — Mac IP banned there); NCAAB/NCAAF are snapshot-only for lines; NHL flags say "goalie unconfirmed".
