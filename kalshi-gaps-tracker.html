Markouts (60-second)

Markout = later ask minus flag ask, in cents. Positive = the price moved in the flagger's favour after the flag (the edge was real; the market corrected toward fair). Negative = adverse selection — the flagged "cheap" price kept getting cheaper, i.e. the book was the stale side, not Kalshi. The paper taker's markout is measured on its own re-checked ask, so it can differ from the scanner's flag by a cent (e.g. BKN/TOR: scanner flagged 46c, taker re-checked 45c).

How to read this page. A gap means Kalshi is selling one side at least 3.0c cheaper than the book's devigged fair price. About 1.7c of that is Kalshi's 7% fee, so the net edge is roughly 1.3c. A gap is NOT an arb — the side still has to win. The paper taker logs what it would have bought (10 contracts, $35/trade cap, 5/day, $100/day, pregame only, gap re-checked live at 3.0c or more). Nothing here places a real order; live trading needs a separate tap and a week of positive paper results first.
