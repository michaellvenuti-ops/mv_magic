How it works (Logic L2 — synthetic hold / chopping the hold). The market is built from the best price on each side across books (full ladder). Synthetic hold = sum of the best prices' implied probabilities − 1. A market flags only when the synthetic hold is near 0% (≤ 0.5%) AND the best price beats the consensus (median implied probability of the other books on that side) by ≥ 5 points of edge — with the hold chopped, a genuine edge tips the bet positive. A negative synthetic hold is arbitrage: logged as an arb-candidate and routed to the existing arb handling, not duplicated here as a paper flag. Markets with hold 0.5–4% and no edge are listed as chopped-but-no-edge shopping reference. Paper only — $100 flat, graded the next morning. Lines: DarkHorse snapshot ladder when live; until then the HaterPicks full ladder (placeable books), labelled on every flag.
