How it works (Mack, Vol. 2 — NHL expected goals). Shot data: MoneyPuck team xG (xGoalsFor / xGoalsAgainst, all situations, 2025 regular season). Each team's xG rate is shrunk 3 games toward the league mean. Goal expectancy: home xGF × away xGA / league xG per game (and vice versa), home-ice factor 1.03 / 0.98. Win/total probabilities come from independent Poisson goal distributions; regulation ties split 50/50 (OT/shootout coin flip). A side flags when the model's edge over the book's implied probability is ≥ 5%. Paper only — $100 flat per flag at the posted price, graded the next morning. Lines: DarkHorse snapshot when live; until then HaterPicks NHL game pages (best price per side, labelled on every flag) — NHL totals are likely not in the snapshot until the Browse Odds probe lands. v1 limits: no starting-goalie adjustment (goalies are announced after the 16:00 scan — every flag says "goalie unconfirmed"); no shot-level xG fit yet (team xG totals only); back-to-back teams are noted, not adjusted.
