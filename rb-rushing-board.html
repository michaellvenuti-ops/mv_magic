How the method works — and its limits

Projection. Projected rushing yards = the RB's average rushing attempts per game (2026 season to date, per game played) × the opposing defense's rushing yards per attempt allowed (opponent rushing yards allowed ÷ opponent rushing attempts against). Example from the reel: 12 att/game × 5.0 opp YPA = 60 projected yards. The RB's own yards-per-carry is deliberately NOT blended in — that is Frank's pure formula.

Chet's display choices (Frank states none of these): a flag when the absolute gap vs the book's main rushing-yards line is 10+ yards, in either direction (projection above the line = Over lean; below = Under lean); an RB is projected only with 4+ games played AND 8+ rushing attempts per game; stats are season-to-date from Pro Football Reference; book lines are FanDuel's main lines. Confirmed flags use FanDuel Research lines only — roundup lines are shown as tentative, never as confirmed flags.

This is a crude estimator — it ignores the RB's own yards-per-carry, game script, injuries, and offensive line play. A flag is a lean to investigate, not a pick. The reel makes NO win-rate or profit claim for this method, so there is no creator claim to test — the board's own grading below is the record.
