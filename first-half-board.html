First Half Board

Weekly NFL alternate first-half strategy from @linemakersports: bet UNDER 28.5 first-half points when the head-to-head history says 29+ first-half points is rare. Updated every Sunday morning; prior weeks stay below as history, with grading.

How it works — and the two corrections

For each game, pull the head-to-head meetings over the last 4-5 seasons and count how often the first-half total went OVER 28.5 points. A qualifier is a matchup whose over-28.5 rate is 25% or lower across at least 6 meetings (a display rule — the reel states no cutoff; its own Packers-Vikings example, 20%, qualifies). The play is UNDER 28.5 first-half points, typically priced -300 to -500.

Correction 1 — price beats win rate: at -500 the breakeven win rate is 83.3%, at -400 it is 80.0%, at -300 it is 75.0%. An 80% win rate LOSES money at -500 and only breaks even at -400, so the reel's win-rate claim only profits at the cheap end of its quoted range. Grading here is by money ($100 flat at the recorded price), not win rate alone. Correction 2 — small samples: a 20% rate on 8-10 meetings is noisy, and matchups with under 6 meetings in the window show their rate but never qualify.
