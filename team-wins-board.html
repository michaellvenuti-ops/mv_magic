How to read the middles: a middle pairs an Over at one book with an Under at another (here the Under is a Kalshi No contract, which pays if the team finishes under the threshold). If the final win count lands between the two lines, both legs win; outside it, exactly one wins, so the "miss" is the same either way. Stakes are balanced on ~$200 total. Kalshi charges small trading fees, so real land/miss is a touch worse than shown.
